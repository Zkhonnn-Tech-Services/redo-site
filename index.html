About ReDo

Giving Waste a Second Life

ReDo is a sustainable manufacturing business that transforms discarded garlic peels and recycled paper pulp into useful, eco-friendly non-food products.

Our goal is to give waste materials a second life while creating affordable, functional, and creative products for everyday use.
